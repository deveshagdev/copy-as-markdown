A Practical Guide to Pour-Over Coffee

By Sam Rivera · 8 min read

Pour-over is the simplest way to make a genuinely good cup at home. You need hot water, a filter cone, freshly ground beans and about four minutes. Everything else is fine-tuning.

What you need

  • A cone dripper and paper filters
  • A burr grinder — blade grinders give an uneven grind
  • A kettle, ideally with a gooseneck spout
  • A kitchen scale that reads to 0.1 g

Ratios that work

CupsCoffeeWaterBrew time
115 g250 g2:45–3:15
230 g500 g3:30–4:00
345 g750 g4:00–4:30

The method

  1. Rinse the filter with hot water and discard it.
  2. Add the coffee and pour twice its weight in water to bloom for 30 seconds.
  3. Pour the rest in slow circles, keeping the bed level.
  4. Let it drain completely, then swirl and serve.

If it tastes sour, grind finer. If it tastes bitter, grind coarser. Change one thing at a time.
